How It Works

Our admission process, start to finish

01

Application

Submit the admission form at the school office with your documents.

02

Document Check

The office verifies your B-Form and previous school record.

03

Seat Availability

Confirmed against open seats in the relevant grade.

04

Headmaster Approval

Final admission is approved by the Headmaster's office.

05

Fund Deposit

Pay the nominal admission fund at the school bank account.

06

Enrollment

Collect your admission slip and join your class.

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • Open to boys resident in Dadu and surrounding areas
  • Grade 1: children must be at least 5 years old
  • Grades 2 and above: previous school record required

Documents Required

  • Child's B-Form (NADRA)
  • Parent/guardian CNIC copy
  • Last school's progress report or transfer certificate
  • Two passport-size photographs
Important Dates

Key admissions dates

Fee Structure

Government-subsidized, low-cost education

As a Government of Sindh school, tuition at GBHS Pilot Dadu follows the official Sindh Education Department fee policy — tuition itself is free. Families pay only small, one-time or annual charges:

Frequently Asked Questions

Common admissions questions
